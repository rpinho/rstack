# Outreach: draft, lint, approve, send

Use `rh outreach --help` for the local lifecycle. Draft and lint privately, then get the operator's approval of the exact final bytes before sending through an authorized tool. `approve` requires a current lint PASS hash and approval quote. Byte changes invalidate it. The [email hook](../tools/email-voice-gate.py) checks the submitted body against the ledger, so verify the recipient separately. Set `RH_OUTREACH_GATE=enforce` to deny unapproved email. Voice errors deny in either mode. The X gate (`xr` and the browser hook) denies by default, with explicit `RH_X_GATE=warn` for warning mode.

- `RH_CONTACTS` points to a private JSON array with unique case-insensitive `name` strings and optional `language` (e.g. `pt`). Keep the contact file and draft bodies out of git. Ledger metadata rejects email addresses. Draft files must live outside temporary directories. `RH_DRAFT_ARCHIVE` defaults to `~/.local/state/research-harness/drafts` (0600 files) and stores content-addressed drafts. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).
- `RH_VOICE_COMMAND` is a shell-quoted argv, run without a shell, reading the draft on stdin. A missing checker, nonzero exit or `FAIL:` refuses lint. Social replies append `--reply`. `OUTREACH.toml` controls questions and follow-ups. `science_checks = true` runs `RH_SCIENCE_CHECK_COMMAND` on JSON with `text`, `language`, `claims` and `attachments` using the same failure convention. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).
- For non-English recipients, write ordinary prose including opening and closing in their language, technical paragraphs and attachments in English. Lint warns on English ordinary prose, and reports positions for passages it can classify. [mixed_language.py](../harness/mixed_language.py).
- `--claim DIR` attaches evidence. With `require_claims = true`, claims are mandatory. Configure `recheck_roots` to find independent `RECHECK.csv` rows with `id` (claim directory basename), `rerun`, `verdict` and optional `utc`, `date` or `checked_utc` (file mtime fallback). Lint refuses missing, failed or dropped rechecks. Evidence in directories containing `pod_*` or `pod-*` requires a recheck after configured `pod_incident_utc`, and warns if it is unset. [claim_evidence.py](../harness/claim_evidence.py).
- Keep provider, infrastructure and incident details out of email, `x_dm` and `linkedin`. For a subject the recipient raised, `rh outreach draft --asked-about TERM` downgrades that `OPS_DETAIL` match to a warning. `ops_mentions.terms(text)` returns allowed term names from the incoming text. [ops_mentions.py](../harness/ops_mentions.py).
- `RH_VOICE_BANKS` lists the author's private language-tagged banks. List borrowed lines in `DRAFT.cuts` as `BANK:LINE`. `--incoming FILE` supplies words for subject swaps. `voice_cuts_min_words` and `voice_cuts_min_pairs` default to 0.70 and 0.40. `voice_cuts = "auto"` follows the banks, `"on"` requires them, and `"off"` disables cut matching. `rh voice-cuts DRAFT [--incoming FILE]` records a normalized-text pass in `RH_VOICE_CUTS_STATE` for the email hook. Retire bank lines with `voice-retired.json` beside the banks file or `RH_VOICE_RETIRED`. [voice_cuts.py](../harness/voice_cuts.py).
- Use singular first person in English even with cuts off (`VOICE_PLURAL`). Family exceptions need `WE-OK: <reason>` in `DRAFT.cuts` and a recorded pass for `voice-cuts plural TEXT`. Portuguese and capitalized `US` are exempt. [voice_cuts.py](../harness/voice_cuts.py).
- Use `sent --utc` to backdate a send of approved bytes. For a message already sent without approval, `rh outreach record-sent LANE ID --message-id M --via api --utc WHEN --reason WHY` keeps lint findings as warnings and gives the reply a record to attach to. Only use it for a past, unapproved send. Use `rh outreach adopt LANE ID --kind K --reason R` for hand-logged rows with `sent_at` and no status. Both commands are bookkeeping. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).

## Reciprocate

Use `rh outreach draft ... --incoming FILE` for replies, including `--requested` drafts and contacts whose turn is ours. Match language, greeting, sign-off, warmth, questions, family and emoticons. Length options are `reciprocate_min_ratio` (0.7), `reciprocate_max_ratio` (1.6), `reciprocate_max_ratio_answering` (2.0), `reciprocate_short_words` (40) and `reciprocate_floor_cap` (120). `--short-ok` takes the owner's words. Review tone against the incoming message before approving. [reciprocate.py](../harness/reciprocate.py), [test_reciprocate.py](../harness/tests/test_reciprocate.py).

## Science emails

`science_email_rules = true` in `OUTREACH.toml` requires a question first unless answering one, a line about the sender (`life_words`), proof that someone uses the code for bug reports (`users`, a checked `--target`, or `--users-override`), the meaning of a tighter number and a call offer after a reply (`call_link`). `--opener-ok`, `--life-ok`, `--meaning-ok` and `--nocall-ok` take the owner's words. Wait for a reply before sending another result unless `--requested` or the contact has `cadence: fast`. [science_email.py](../harness/science_email.py).

## Email to a friend

`friend_groups` defaults to `["friend"]`. `friend_max_words` (60), `friend_max_paragraphs` (2) and the list ban apply. If the contact wants one sentence or noticed AI, use `--owner-wrote` with the owner's words for a draft they wrote. For friends, the life line, meaning, call offer and reciprocate length floor are optional. Keep the question first. [friend_email.py](../harness/friend_email.py).

## Bug-hunt lane

See the [example config](../examples/friends-lane/OUTREACH.toml) and [test_bughunt.py](../harness/tests/test_bughunt.py).

1. `rh bughunt target-check LANE OWNER/REPO [--package INDEX:NAME]` checks active maintenance and outside use and records `TARGETS.jsonl`. Use `pypi:`, `cran:`, `bioconda:` or `conda-forge:`. Configure `target_max_age_days`, `target_check_valid_days`, `target_required_kinds`, `usage_window_days`, `usage_min_outside_accounts`, `usage_min_stars`, `usage_min_downloads_month` and `usage_min_conda_downloads`. Draft with `--target OWNER/REPO`. `--target-override REASON` and `--users-override "their words"` record why the corresponding check should warn. [bughunt.py](../harness/bughunt.py).
2. `emails_per_contact_per_day = 1` limits sends across lanes. `rh outreach batch LANE CONTACT --file OUT` combines drafts and drops their old ledger entries. Edit out the merge marker before lint. `--requested "their words"` downgrades the daily and recent-contact checks to warnings. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).
3. `sent`, `reply` and `bounce` overlay contact status through `RH_CONTACT_UPDATES` (default `contact-updates.jsonl`, mode 0600). `rh outreach contact pending` shows edits for the contacts owner. `rh outreach contact engagement NAME TEXT --source S` records engagement wording. `rh outreach contact find NAME [--url PAGE] [--record ADDRESS]` looks up a sourced address. Respect engagement preferences and `BOUNCED` addresses. [contacts_ledger.py](../harness/contacts_ledger.py).
4. `rh outreach contact scrutiny NAME extra` requires `extra_rechecks` (default 2) independent `--recheck DIR` verdicts (`VERDICT.md`, `VERDICTS.md`, `RECHECK.csv` or accepted `verdict.json`) and `rh outreach review LANE ID --by WHO --verdict pass` on the final bytes. Configure `required_questions`, `required_question_names` and `required_questions_kinds` for mandatory questions. `rh outreach contact answered NAME QUESTION --source S` records an answer. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).
5. Use repeatable `--kind paper|code-bug|code-speedup|other` as appropriate. `rh outreach set-kind LANE ID KIND --reason R` fixes historical bookkeeping. `rh outreach stats [LANE] [--contact NAME] [--json]` reports counts and replies. Use `--aggregate` for publication without names. [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py).
6. For `--speedup DIR`, supply `SPEEDUP.json` with a pinned commit, local clone, patch, output command and files, optional numerical tolerance, runs and measured hotspot. Run `rh bughunt speedup-verify LANE DIR` within the third-party execution boundary. It writes `VERIFY.json` after comparing outputs and measuring median wall time and peak memory. It refuses on controller (`RH_NO_LOCAL_COMPUTE=1`). Use `speedup_min_ratio` and `speedup_min_seconds` for the bar. Refresh stale evidence. Check overlapping upstream work and record `--overlap-checked "how"`. Extra scrutiny requires a second separate verification. [bughunt.py](../harness/bughunt.py), [third_party.py](../harness/tools/third_party.py).

## Bug-hunt referees

- `rh bughunt claims-check LANE claims.json` checks `REFUTED.jsonl`. A refuted location needs a changed `file_sha256` or `differs_from` with the old fingerprint and reason. Use `REFUTED` for a claim the evidence disproves. Use `BLOCKED` with `needs` and `check` for a missing fact (`BLOCKED.jsonl`). [bug_verdicts.py](../harness/bug_verdicts.py).
- `rh bughunt referee-prompt LANE claims.json WORKDIR` uses the [standard prompt](../harness/templates/bug_referee.md). Declare `hunter.model` and `hunter.family` or `--hunter-family`. Use a referee from a different model family. Optionally provide `--plants`. The key in `PLANTS.jsonl` stays sealed from the referee.
- `rh bughunt verdicts LANE verdict.json [--receipt JOB.log.selection.json]` checks schema `bug-verdicts/1`, claim completeness, planted negatives and family. Use the launch receipt or `RH_LAUNCH_RECEIPTS` to resolve family. Redo a malformed verdict with the referee. Lint requires an accepted structured verdict for each such `--recheck` and a third referee to resolve contradictory verdicts. `rh bughunt referees LANE` shows confirm and plant-catch rates. [bug_verdicts.py](../harness/bug_verdicts.py), [test_bug_verdicts.py](../harness/tests/test_bug_verdicts.py).
- `rh bughunt coverage LANE record FILE` records module × bug-class coverage. Repeats warn without counting. `rh bughunt coverage LANE show OWNER/REPO` is done after two rounds with no new candidate or deferral in the last. [bug_coverage.py](../harness/bug_coverage.py).

## Growing a small account on X

Reply with something substantive under larger accounts at 0–500 followers. Above that, post a strong original with a figure for each real result. Keep playful replies short and propose on-camera recordings to the owner. [Bier's growth strategy, 2022-03-03](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1499452301185929216), [short replies, 2026-02-05](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2019346324173537337), [video, 2026-04-12](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2043404323485589763).

- `x_original_gap_minutes` (120 or legacy `RH_X_MIN_GAP_MINUTES`) spaces originals across lanes. `x_reply_gap_minutes` (3) spaces actual reply attempts. Filler-only originals fail lint.
- `x_reply_human_approval = true` by default. Get a person's approval in their own words for each round of replies. Only the owner may change this setting in `OUTREACH.toml`.
- `rh outreach retract LANE ID --reason TEXT [--replaced-by URL]` records a post already deleted. If public oEmbed is unavailable, use `--deleted-confirmed` with the verification. The retained record releases the posting slot.

Enforcement and tests: [outreach.py](../harness/outreach.py), [test_outreach.py](../harness/tests/test_outreach.py), [test_outreach_gate.py](../harness/tests/test_outreach_gate.py).
